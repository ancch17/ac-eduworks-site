About

Dr Andrew Chew

Principle Consultant & Trainer · Generative AI · Digital Marketing · Corporate Leadership

Passion to Empower

A Career Built on Teaching & Innovation

Dr Andrew Chew is an accomplished marketing professional and educator with over 30 years of experience across Fortune 500 companies — including Sony, Dell, Motorola, Signify, and Schneider Electric — as well as academia at NUS, Manchester University, Murdoch University, and Republic Polytechnic.

Since January 2025, Andrew has been working full-time as an Associate Lecturer at leading institutions in Singapore — NTUC Learning Hub, NTU, and SUSS — focused entirely on AI & Digital Marketing training.

Dr Andrew is also an excellent communicator who won numerous Toastmasters competitions, including the prestigious District 80 Humorous Speech Championship in 2007.
